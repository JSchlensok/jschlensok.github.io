I am a bioinformatics researcher developing machine learning methods to deepen our understanding of how proteins shape life.

Currently, I work as a scientific employee at Burkhard Rost's lab at the TUM and a visiting researcher in Michael Heinzinger's group at Helmholtz Munich.

My main research areas include variant effect prediction and protein engineering using protein language models.
